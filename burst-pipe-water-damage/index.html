Burst pipe: what to do first.

A burst pipe is an emergency, and our 24/7 line is open for it. Find the main shutoff and close it, open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the line, and keep power off in any wet area unless you can reach the panel without standing in water.

Then call. Water from a supply line is clean at first, which is the best case for saving materials, but that window narrows as the water soaks drywall and insulation.

Night and weekend bursts are common because plumbing fails under pressure whenever it fails. Same-day response is the goal when crews are available, and the person who answers will tell you what to expect.

Why a burst pipe does more damage than it looks like.

The visible puddle is only part of the story. Water runs down inside the wall cavity, soaks the insulation, drips onto the floor below and travels along joists to rooms you may not have checked.

A pressurized line can release a great deal before anyone notices, especially when the break is in a wall, an attic or a basement ceiling. That is why we scan with a thermal camera and probe with a moisture meter instead of trusting what the surface shows.

How we clean up and dry after a pipe break.

  1. Confirm the shutoff.

    We make sure the supply is closed so the loss is not still growing, and we note where the break is for your plumber.

  2. Extract and open up.

    Standing water is extracted and wet drywall or ceiling is cut back along clean lines so cavities can dry.

  3. Dry with equipment.

    Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run while we log moisture readings each visit.

  4. Hand off to repair.

    When the structure reaches drying goals the rebuild can start, either with us or your own contractor.

Industry standard behind burst pipe drying.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for professional water damage restoration. For a burst pipe it guides how we classify the loss by how much of the structure is wet and how long the water has been sitting.

We mention the standard because it explains why drying is measured instead of guessed. It is an industry document, and citing it is not a statement that we hold a certificate.

What affects the cost of burst pipe cleanup.

What moves the price.

The main drivers are how long the water ran, how many floors it reached, whether ceilings came down and how much drywall and insulation had to be removed. A break found in minutes costs less to dry than one that ran overnight.

Plumbing repair is separate from restoration. We explain our scope and price before work starts and note the break location for whoever fixes the pipe.

Insurance notes for a burst pipe.

Sudden pipe discharge is a loss many homeowner policies address, but terms differ, so ask your insurer what applies and tell them it is water damage. Photograph the break, the wet areas and any ruined belongings before cleanup, and keep damaged items until the insurer says otherwise.

If the cause is cold weather.

Many breaks in this part of Michigan follow a hard freeze. If the pipe froze and then split, the frozen pipe page explains the thaw and the water that comes after. Leaks that start at a tank connection are covered under water heater leak cleanup, and wet ceilings below the break are handled in ceiling water damage repair.

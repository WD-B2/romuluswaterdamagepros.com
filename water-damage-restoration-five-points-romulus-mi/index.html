Five Points and downtown Romulus: where the old center meets the new.

Five Points grew out of Pullen's Corners, the settlement where Jenks Pullen and his sons put down roots in 1833 and where the community was platted in 1836 at Ozga and Goddard roads. That corner later gave the whole township its name, and today Goddard Road is the center of downtown Romulus.

The area is a mix of older storefronts, small businesses and nearby homes. The city rebuilt the downtown streets with new infrastructure and brick pavement, and the Downtown Development Authority has bought, repaired and leased older buildings to new tenants.

For a restoration call, that mix matters. A burst line can be in a family home one block and in a storefront or a rented unit the next, so the first question we ask is who owns the space and who needs to approve the work.

Stop the source.

Mind the power.

Keep power off in wet areas only if you can reach the panel without standing in water. Stay out of rooms where water touches outlets.

Move valuables and furniture legs out of the water and photograph the damage for your insurance claim.

What the weather has done in Romulus.

Storms can overwhelm local drains. On July 28, 2025 the National Weather Service recorded 3.31 inches of rain in Romulus in about an hour and a half, with flooded roads, stranded vehicles and downed power lines reported in the area.

That event is a regional weather record and not a claim about any one street in Five Points. It is a reminder to know where your shutoff is, keep photos of your belongings and call early when water enters a building.

Documentation and insurance.

The Michigan Department of Insurance and Financial Services says flood damage is not covered by a standard homeowners policy. Limited water or sewer back-up coverage can be added by endorsement, so ask your agent what your policy says.

Tell your insurer you have water damage, photograph everything and keep damaged items until the insurer says you can discard them. We provide a written scope and moisture readings that you can hand to an adjuster.

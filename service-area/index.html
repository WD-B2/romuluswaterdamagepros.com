How we decide whether we can reach you.

Distance and traffic matter, especially during storms when many calls arrive at once. Romulus sits next to I-94 and I-275, which helps crews reach the towns around us, but road closures can change that.

The July 28, 2025 storm put 3.31 inches of rain on Romulus in about an hour and a half, and roads flooded across the area. After events like that, we tell callers honestly how long reaching them is likely to take instead of making a promise we cannot keep.

What sets each nearby town apart.

Belleville wraps around Belleville Lake and the Huron River dam, so lakeside and basement water are common concerns. New Boston is an unincorporated community in Huron Charter Township, where basements near the Huron River lead the calls.

Inkster sits along the Rouge River, so basement flooding and sewage backups come up most. Garden City, a city since 1933, has many older homes, so aging water heaters and appliance lines are frequent causes.

Each town has its own page with a ZIP list, local facts and the services people there ask about most.

What to tell us when you are outside Romulus.

Give the street address and the nearest cross street, and say which town you are in. Several nearby communities share ZIP codes or mailing names, so the street matters more than the town label.

Tell us where the water is coming from and whether it is still running. A person will use that to decide what to ask next and how to explain the plan.

Roads, landmarks and how we get around.

Romulus sits on I-94 and I-275, with Wayne Road, Merriman Road and Eureka Road carrying much of the local traffic. Detroit Metropolitan Airport has its own ZIP code, 48242, which is used for the airport itself, while residential and business addresses in the city use 48174 and 48184.

Crews choose routes based on where you are and what is open. Flooded underpasses and closed roads after storms can change the plan, and the person on the phone will explain what that means for you.

When to call instead of waiting.

If water is still moving, if it reached an outlet or if it came from a drain or the river, call right away. Waiting lets moisture soak into drywall, subfloor and insulation, and that makes the repair larger.

If you are not sure whether we cover your street, call anyway. The person on the line will ask for your address, the rooms affected and the source, and then explain what happens next.

What to have ready when you call.

Have your street address, the nearest cross street and a phone number where you can be reached. If it is safe, note which rooms are wet and roughly how deep the water is.

If you have a landlord, property manager or insurance contact, keep their details nearby. You do not need a policy number to start, but it helps when paperwork comes later.

Places inside Romulus we know.

Inside the city we work across areas such as Five Points, the Eureka and Middlebelt area, Preston, the Vining Road Development District, the East Airport area and the neighborhoods west of Huron River Drive. Ask about your specific street when you call.

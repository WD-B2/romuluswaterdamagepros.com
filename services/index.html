All services.

Pick the cause, find the page.

Water damage restoration is not one job. A split pipe, a flooded basement and a wet ceiling all call for different equipment and different decisions, so we organized our services by cause. Find the group that matches what you are seeing, and the page explains what we do, what changes the price and what to do while you wait.

If you are not sure which fits, call the 24/7 emergency line and describe it in your own words. We will point you to the right place and start planning while you are still on the phone.

Pipes and plumbing.

Plumbing failures release clean water at first, which gives restoration the best chance of saving materials if it starts quickly. These pages cover pipes that split, freeze, leak at the tank or fail under the slab.

  • Burst Pipe Water DamageCleanup and drying after a supply line splits and floods a wall, floor or ceiling.
  • Frozen Pipe Water DamageDrying and repair help once a frozen line thaws and releases water inside the house.
  • Water Heater Leak CleanupCleanup after a tank leak or failed fitting leaves water across a utility room or basement.
  • Slab Leak Water DamageMoisture recovery once a leak under the concrete slab soaks flooring and walls.
Call (844) 950-6900

Appliances and HVAC.

Appliances and air handlers leak slowly more often than they burst, so the damage tends to hide under cabinets and above ceilings. Start here if the source is a machine or a kitchen fixture.

  • Appliance Leak Water DamageWater removal after a washer, dishwasher or refrigerator line lets go.
  • AC Leak Water DamageDrying and repair when a clogged condensate line or failed pan drips into the ceiling.
  • Kitchen Water DamageDrying and rebuild for cabinets, floors and walls after a sink, dishwasher or supply leak.

Weather and building envelope.

Water that arrives from outside or from above is the least predictable, and it often includes more than one problem at once. These pages cover storms, floods, ceilings, basements and crawl spaces.

  • Ceiling Water Damage RepairDry out, cut back and rebuild ceilings stained or sagging from a roof or upstairs leak.
  • Storm Water DamageCleanup after wind driven rain, failed roofs and storm runoff soak the building.
  • Flood Damage CleanupRemoval and drying for water that comes in from outside, from heavy rain to rising runoff.
  • Basement Water DamageWater removal, drying and cleanup for finished and unfinished basements.
  • Crawl Space Water DamageWater removal and drying for crawl spaces holding standing water or wet insulation.

Water removal and drying.

Sometimes the cause matters less than the job itself. These pages cover pulling water out, drying a structure with measured readings and handling contaminated water safely.

  • Water ExtractionPumps and extractors that pull standing water out before it reaches the subfloor.
  • Structural DryingControlled drying of framing, subfloor and wall cavities with measured moisture readings.
  • Sewage Backup CleanupProtective cleanup and disinfection after wastewater backs up through floor drains or fixtures.

Floors and walls.

Floors and walls are what you see and what you pay to replace. These pages explain what can be dried and saved and what usually cannot.

  • Hardwood Floor Water DamageMoisture control and repair options for cupped, buckled or stained wood floors.
  • Carpet Water DamageCarpet and pad drying, cleaning or replacement decisions after water gets in.
  • Wall and Drywall Water DamageMoisture checks, drywall removal and repair for walls soaked by leaks and spills.

Commercial.

Businesses have downtime, tenants and landlords to think about, so this service gets its own page.

When two services seem to fit.

Many losses touch more than one page. A pipe that splits in an exterior wall may need burst pipe cleanup, ceiling repair and structural drying, and a storm can bring a wet basement along with a roof leak.

The Rouge River and Inkster flooding.

Inkster is a city of about 26,000 people on the Rouge River, roughly 14 miles southwest of downtown Detroit. In 2026 Wayne County Parks began a $3.5 million, federally funded project at Inkster Park along the river to reduce neighborhood flooding and restore habitat across 17 acres, and residents told reporters the park floods at least once a year.

That is a useful reminder for homeowners nearby. A river that rises and rushes through a floodplain can send water toward basements, and one resident said flooding backed water into a basement about five years earlier.

What to tell us on the call.

A few details speed things up. Tell us your address, where the water came from, which rooms are wet and whether power or sewage is involved. If you know the floor type and whether there is a basement or crawl space, say so.

If you are a tenant or a business, mention that too, because it changes who approves the work. We will ask the rest.

Before you call.

Stop the source.

Close the main valve or the shutoff for the fixture if it is safe.

Mind the power.

Keep power off in wet areas unless you can reach the panel dry.

Lift valuables.

Move small items and furniture legs off wet floors.

Take photos.

Photograph the damage before anything is moved.

Email and general questions.

For paperwork or general questions that are not urgent, write to contact@romuluswaterdamagepros.com and we will reply during business hours. For anything involving active water, call instead.
